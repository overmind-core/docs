Overmind ingests OpenTelemetry traces, scores them as they arrive, turns them into curated datasets, to be used for evaluations, optimisations and model training benchmarked against what you currently have.

The platform can be used as [cloud hosted](https://console.overmindlab.ai) or [self-hosted](/platform/self-hosting) with Docker Compose.

Overmind is built on top of standard protocols so no matter what agent you use, you can use Overmind to improve it, with little to no effort.

What Overmind does:
- [Capability scan](/core/capabilities): Scanning your codebase for AI agents and different agentic capabilities.
- [Live scoring](/agent-testing/trace-scoring): every trace is carved into task executions and judged against the agent's eval set
- [Datasets from traces](/core/datasets): traces from real environments become eval and training data, no synthetic data, no data poisoning, no hallucinations.
- [Evaluations](/agent-testing/eval): your in house quality control loop, measured using a typed score, ensuring the agent is always improving.
- [Optimiser](/agent-testing/optimisers): An AI Engineer running experiments on your repository, trying to improve the agent's performance while adhering to your quality control loop.
- [Models](/models/training): Finetuned models that you own, built on top of your custom data.
- [Inference](/models/inference): OpenAI-compatible API for your trained models and frontier models
