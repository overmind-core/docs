## Live and controlled evaluation

Enabled eval matrix rows score matching spans. A deterministic row runs a constrained Python check; an `llm` row states an expectation judged by Jev. Finished traces can also receive trajectory measurements on their root span. Scores append history and remain inspectable with their reasons and errors.

A controlled evaluation freezes a matrix and a dataset cell. Each target gets its own run in a shared group. Recorded targets use saved outputs; generated targets request new outputs. Comparison pairs rows both sides scored.

A dataset row represents the capability's answer, so answer-level checks apply even when their live `span_name` names the function producing that answer. Tool-only checks do not automatically become checks of the final dataset answer.

## What a score does not establish

Abstention means a check did not produce a judgement. A technical error means judging failed. A pending row has not finished. None is a quality zero or a pass.

The same principle applies to generation: a token-limited response can be transport-successful while incomplete. Read coverage and degraded outcomes before comparing means. A high mean over the few completed cases can hide a weak overall result.

Jev supplies the expectation decision. A separate generative model can explain a low score. Selecting an explanation judge does not replace the scoring engine, and a provider failure does not silently switch to a generative grader.

## Decisions need their own measurements

A probability model predicts over options supplied with the question. Full distributions, hard observations and ordinal means carry different information. Their metrics must preserve that distinction.

Native comparisons freeze suites and optionally fit calibration before final scoring. Raw and calibrated results remain separate. A calibrated score on calibration data is in-sample evidence, not final generalisation performance.

Model speed is another experiment: it depends on input shape, concurrency, batching, warm state and measurement boundaries. Quality results alone cannot establish serving throughput or an all-in cost saving.

## Improvement is a comparison claim

Keep the task, data roles, model identities, checks and coverage visible. Development gains can guide another experiment. A defensible final result requires an appropriate held-out population and an honest account of missing or incompatible outputs.
